# Sound credits

`trexRoar.wav` and `trexVictoryRoar.wav` are clips of "T-Rex calls" by CaveboyTup, from https://opengameart.org/content/t-rex-calls,
released under CC0 (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
It is trimmed, faded and volume-matched. The original was layered from CC0 alligator, lion and elk samples.
