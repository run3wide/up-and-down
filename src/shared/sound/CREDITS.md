# Sound credits

`trexRoar.wav` and `trexVictoryRoar.wav` are clips of "T-Rex calls" by CaveboyTup, from https://opengameart.org/content/t-rex-calls,
released under CC0 (public domain): https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
It is trimmed, faded and volume-matched. The original was layered from CC0 alligator, lion and elk samples.

`winner.ogg` is the "Winner" line from "Voiceover Pack: Fighter" by Kenney (https://kenney.nl), via
https://opengameart.org/content/voiceover-pack-fighter-40-taunts, released under CC0 (public domain).
The game plays it slowed down to make a deep dinosaur voice.

The animal sounds in `animals/` are real recordings:
- `Moo`, `Baa`, `Bleat`, `Meow`, `Bark`, `Squeal` (the pig), `Whinny` and `Peeps` come from "Baby Animals - Sounds Pack" by Technopeasant
  (https://opengameart.org/content/baby-animals-sounds-pack), CC0 (public domain). Its clips come from CC0 Freesound recordings.
- `Ribbit.mp3` is one of the "Ribbit Frog Sounds" (https://opengameart.org/content/ribbit-frog-sounds), CC0 (public domain).
- `Buzz.ogg` is "Bombus buzz" by Mysid, a bumblebee recorded in Southern Finland, public domain
  (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bombus_buzz.ogg).
